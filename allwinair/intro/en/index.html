Frequently asked questions

What is ALLWIN AIR's business model?
A B2B ancillary gateway (AGS) that connects airlines and ancillary suppliers with travel agencies and OTAs through one integration. ALLWIN AIR runs integration, purchase screens, payment, CS, settlement and maintenance, and both suppliers and sellers earn when a sale happens.
Does ALLWIN AIR sell directly to consumers?
No. ALLWIN AIR is B2B and serves travelers inside its partner sellers' booking flows.
Who should partner with ALLWIN AIR?
Airlines and ancillary suppliers that want more sales channels, and travel agencies, OTAs and travel platforms that want revenue beyond tickets — including companies outside Korea that want to sell Korean airline ancillaries.

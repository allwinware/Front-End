Why niches become expansion

  • Sellers stay the same. New products appear on screens that are already connected, with almost no development.
  • Suppliers gain every seller at once. eSIM, pickup and airport service providers connect to ALLWIN AIR once and reach all partner sellers.
  • The booking drives recommendations. Itinerary and party data put the right product in front of the right traveler, so conversion is high.
  • Inbound, too. We work with overseas partners to connect visitors to Korea with eSIM, airport pickup and airport services.

Frequently asked questions

What services is ALLWIN AIR adding?
Block Seat, SIM/eSIM, home–airport pickup, airport duty-free and airport facility services are in development, and relaunching airport lounges and in-flight duty-free is under discussion.
Do sellers need to redevelop when services are added?
Mostly not. New products are added to screens that are already connected, so there is little extra development.
Can we partner as an ancillary supplier?
Yes. eSIM, pickup, airport service and duty-free suppliers can connect to ALLWIN AIR once and sell through all partner sellers.

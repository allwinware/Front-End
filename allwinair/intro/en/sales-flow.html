Sales Flows

Three sales flows that plug
into the existing booking path

Sellers do not need to rebuild their booking screens. Ancillaries simply sit where travelers already pass — during booking, right after payment and after ticketing.

Contact partnershipsPartnership info

① My-page purchase

After ticketing, the 'Buy seats/baggage' button on the booking page (My page) opens the ALLWIN AIR ancillary screen. First launched on Interpark international in November 2019, it recorded KRW 53 million in December 2019 and KRW 92 million in January 2020.

② Bundled purchase

Travelers choose seats, baggage and meals inside the booking flow and pay for the ticket and ancillaries at once. Launched on Interpark international in October 2020.

③ Post-payment purchase

On the payment-complete page, travelers are asked 'Would you like a more comfortable seat?' and go straight to seat and baggage purchase. Launched on Webtour domestic in November 2020.

Frequently asked questions

Which sales flow should a seller choose?
All three can run together. We recommend bundled purchase during booking for the first sale, plus post-payment offers and my-page purchase to catch travelers who skipped it.
What conversion do ALLWIN AIR ancillaries achieve?
In early operation, purchase conversion (items ÷ visitors) was 56.1% on international and 25.2% on domestic flights, far above the 1–2% typical in e-commerce.
Are both domestic and international flights supported?
Yes. Both are supported; available products vary by airline and route.

What happens with direct integration

Every airline has its own API, product set, pricing rules and refund policy for ancillaries. An agency integrating directly must build one integration per airline, rework it whenever an airline changes its system, and handle customer service and settlement itself. Airlines face the same problem with each agency.

What partners do becomes simple

  1. Sign with ALLWIN AIR and choose the model (API integration or ALLWIN AIR purchase screens).
  2. Integrate once and test.
  3. Start selling. New products, airline-side changes, CS and settlement are handled by ALLWIN AIR from then on.

Frequently asked questions

Do we need to build each airline separately?
No. One ALLWIN AIR integration lets you sell ancillaries from every connected airline and supplier. New airlines and products need little or no extra development.
Who handles customer inquiries about ancillaries?
The ALLWIN AIR customer center handles inquiries, changes and refunds from ancillary buyers.
Do we have to build purchase screens?
Your choice. Connect to ALLWIN AIR purchase screens, or integrate by API and build them into your own interface.

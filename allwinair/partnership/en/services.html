Sales models

  1. Bundled purchase — travelers select ancillaries during flight booking and pay once together with the ticket.
  2. Post-payment offer — ancillaries offered right on the ticket payment confirmation page.
  3. My-page purchase — ancillaries purchased any time after ticketing from the booking details page.

We agree on the best fit for each partner, from API integration to ALLWIN AIR–provided purchase screens.

Looking for new ancillary suppliers

Any service travelers need before, during or after their trip is a candidate: eSIM/roaming, airport transport and pickup, airport duty-free, lounges, fast track, baggage delivery and storage, travel insurance, local tours and activities.

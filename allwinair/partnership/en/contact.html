Frequently asked questions

What is ALLWIN AIR?
A South Korean B2B ancillary gateway (AGS) that integrates airline paid ancillary services — seat selection, baggage, meals, priority baggage, golf packages — into travel agency and OTA sales channels.
Can consumers buy directly from ALLWIN AIR?
No. ALLWIN AIR is B2B; travelers purchase ancillaries through our partner airlines' and travel agencies' booking flows.
Which airlines do you connect?
Jin Air, Jeju Air, T'way Air, Air Busan, Air Seoul, Air Premia and Eastar Jet are live; Parata Air and Aero K are onboarding; Korean Air and Asiana Airlines are planned.
What do travel agencies gain?
New ancillary revenue from multiple airlines through one integration, while ALLWIN AIR takes over airline integration, maintenance, customer service and settlement.
What is AI seat recommendation?
It recommends suitable seats from the booking's (PNR) passenger and companion context, so travelers select seats quickly without browsing a seat map.
Can foreign companies partner with you?
Yes. We welcome foreign OTAs and airlines that want Korean airline ancillaries, and businesses serving inbound travelers to Korea. English is fine.
Can we join as an ancillary supplier?
Yes. We are actively seeking eSIM/SIM, airport pickup, airport duty-free, airport facility and lounge suppliers.

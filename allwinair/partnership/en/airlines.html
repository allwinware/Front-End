Value for airlines

More sales channels

Extend ancillary sales from your own website to travel agencies and OTAs.

Less integration work

Reach many agencies through ALLWIN AIR instead of connecting each agency separately.

High-margin revenue

Ancillaries have low cost of sales and are not price-comparison products, making them highly profitable.

Operations handled

Agency integration, display management, customer service and settlement are handled by ALLWIN AIR.

Integration scope

  • Seat selection (AI recommendation), pre-paid baggage, meals, priority baggage, golf package
  • Airline API / NDC connectivity and standardized conversion for each agency
  • Display control per agency/airline, unified sales and payment management, settlement
  • Both international and domestic flights

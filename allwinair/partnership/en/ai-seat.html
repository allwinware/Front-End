AI Seat Recommendation

Seat selection by recommendation,
not by seat map

Conventional seat selection asks travelers to browse a seat map with hundreds of seats. ALLWIN AIR recommends the right seats first based on the booking, so travelers finish in a few taps.

How it works

  1. Read party size, companion type (family, couple, with infant, etc.) and itinerary from the booking (PNR).
  2. Build recommended seat sets considering sitting together and seat attributes such as front row, exit row, window and aisle.
  3. The traveler picks a recommendation, or adjusts it, and pays.

Why it works

  • “Every seat becomes valuable when you give it meaning” — seat benefits are presented to fit the traveler's situation.
  • Quick selection on mobile, no zooming or scrolling through seat maps.
  • Party-level recommendation makes it easy for companions to sit together.
  • Built on our patented technology for PNR-based seat pre-purchase and item recommendation.

Where it can be used

  • Travel agency / OTA web & app
  • Airline websites
  • Airport check-in
  • Other seated venues (arenas, musicals)
